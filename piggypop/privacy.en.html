PiggyPop Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

3. Retention and deletion

  • The online ranking account and nickname are kept until you delete them. Scores are removed when the server cleans up records more than eight weeks after their week ended. Cleanup runs during score submissions, so removal at the exact eight-week mark is not guaranteed.
  • The Operator does not keep its own record of service settings requests.
  • Support emails are kept for up to three years after the request is resolved, to handle disputes, and then deleted.
  • Information stored on your device is deleted when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data in Android settings. On iOS, it is deleted when you delete the app.
  • Information sent to Google Firebase: Analytics user- and event-level data is deleted automatically after at most 14 months under the Google Analytics data retention setting. Aggregated reports are not affected by that setting and may be kept longer. Crashlytics keeps crash information and related identifiers (Crashlytics installation UUID and Firebase installation ID) for 90 days and then starts deleting them. The Firebase installation ID itself and service logs and backups follow Google’s policy.
  • Server records are deleted from the database. Google, Apple, Supabase and Unity set the retention of the information they receive and of their own service logs and backups; the Operator does not.

4. Account deletion

PiggyPop’s online account is an anonymous account used only for the ranking. You can delete it as follows.

  1. In the Game: open Settings and tap Delete Online Ranking Data. The anonymous account and the nickname and ranking scores on the server are deleted together. Game progress on your device stays. After deletion, the Game does not create a new account or send records until you open Online Ranking again.
  2. If you uninstalled the Game or cannot open it: email cs@fiveduckgames.com with the subject “PiggyPop account deletion”. Include your ranking nickname and, if you know them, the weeks and scores you submitted. Account IDs are not shown in the Game and nicknames can repeat, so we may ask for more details. We delete the account without undue delay after confirming it and reply with the result.

Deletion removes the account ID, nickname and ranking scores; the Operator keeps no copy. Service provider logs follow section 3. Game data on your device can be deleted by uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data in Android settings. On iOS, it is deleted when you delete the app.

5. Your rights and choices

  • You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your information at cs@fiveduckgames.com. You can change your nickname yourself in the game settings.
  • Where consent is required, you can change advertising choices in the UMP form and in Ad Privacy Settings in the game settings. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. On iOS, you can decline the tracking request and later change whether PiggyPop may track you in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Firebase in the Android app: where consent is required, Analytics storage and ad-related storage stay denied until the UMP consent process is completed (section 1). You can reset or delete the advertising ID that Analytics uses in Android settings. Crash reporting and remote configuration cannot be turned off in the Game. The Operator does not link Firebase records to your nickname or online ranking account, so account deletion in section 4 does not remove them; they are deleted when the periods in section 3 end.
  • EEA and UK users: the Operator processes information to provide the ranking you ask for (contract), for personalized ads and device access where required (consent), and for security, abuse prevention, service settings and date checks (legitimate interests). You have the rights to data portability, to object and to withdraw consent, and you may complain to your local data protection authority.
  • Users in certain US states: the Operator does not sell personal information for money. To opt out of processing for targeted advertising, use Ad Privacy Settings, shown in the game settings in your region. On iOS, you can also turn tracking off in the tracking setting above.

6. Children

The Game is not designed for children under 13. If we learn that information of a child below the age set by local law (for example, under 14 in the Republic of Korea) was stored in the ranking without a parent’s or guardian’s consent, we delete it without undue delay. Parents and guardians can request deletion as described in section 4.

7. Security

All communication with servers is encrypted with HTTPS. The Game cannot access server tables directly and calls only defined functions, and no server administration key is included in the app. Only the Operator has server administration access.

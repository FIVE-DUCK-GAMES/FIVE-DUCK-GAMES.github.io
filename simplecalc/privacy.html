Simple Calculator Privacy Policy

FIVE DUCK GAMES provides Simple Calculator (com.ClearSky.HuntwithClearSky). Contact: fiveduckgames@gmail.com. Last updated: October 23, 2026.

Simple Calculator does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information or other user data.

1. What the app does with your data

Numbers you enter are used only on your device to show the result and are discarded when you close the app. The app has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. It requests no permissions, including internet access, so it cannot send data off your device. Android backup is turned off for the app.

2. Websites and support

GitHub Pages hosts this policy page; requests may expose your IP address and request metadata to the host. Support emails contain your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send; we keep them as long as needed to resolve your request and delete them on request.

3. Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

4. Changes

We will update this page and the store’s data safety information before we change what the app collects.

2. Retention period

The account identifier and game records are kept until you delete the account. When an in-game account deletion succeeds, they are deleted from the production database immediately; email requests are processed within 7 days of receipt after we verify the requester and the target account, and we reply with the result. Information that remains after deletion, such as other players' Sparring defense records, is explained in the data deletion page. On the Supabase free plan, service logs that the operator can view are kept for 1 day by default. The Company does not export these logs to separate storage. The provider's own security and service operation records are governed by the Supabase Privacy Policy.

For disaster recovery, the Company backs up the server database (account identifier, sign-up time, guest flag and game records). Backups are kept on Company-managed storage with restricted access (in the Republic of Korea) and deleted 30 days after they are made. After you delete your account, its records may remain in backups made before the deletion, but they are not used for any purpose other than recovery and are deleted together with the backup within 30 days. If we ever need to restore from a backup, we delete again the records of accounts for which deletion was requested before putting the data back into service.

Age information, consent records, purchase entitlement records, bonus reward records and appearance selections are stored on the device and are deleted when you delete the app or clear its data. Purchase entitlement records and appearance selections are stored separately from the game account, so they may remain on the device even after you delete the game account.

Google keeps Google Play transaction records and Apple keeps App Store transaction records. The Company can view the order and refund information in Section 1 in Google Play Console and sales information in App Store Connect. Under Article 6 of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce and Article 6 of its Enforcement Decree, the Company keeps the following records.

Destruction procedure and method

The Company destroys personal information without delay once its retention period ends or its purpose has been achieved. Records that must be kept by law are stored separately from other personal information, are not used for anything other than that purpose, and are destroyed when the retention period ends. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered, and backups older than 30 days are deleted as whole folders. Emails and attachments are permanently deleted from the mailbox and the trash. Any printed paper documents are shredded or incinerated.

7. Security measures

All communication with the server is encrypted (HTTPS). On Android, login information on the device is encrypted with an Android Keystore key and kept in storage that is excluded from device backups; on iOS, it is kept in a Keychain item usable only on this device (not synced to other devices). On both platforms, if you delete and reinstall the app, the previous login information is discarded rather than reused. The database applies per-account access restrictions, and backups are kept only on Company-managed storage with restricted access. Purchase receipts are verified on the device — on Android with the Google Play public key, on iOS using Apple-signed transaction information confirmed by StoreKit — and only the SHA-256 hash is stored instead of the raw transaction identifier.

11. Optional rewarded ads and behavioral information

The only ad in the Game is a rewarded ad that you choose to watch after an offline settlement when you want an extra reward. There are no banner or interstitial ads. Not watching ads does not affect the Game or the basic offline reward.

The advertising SDK is connected only for users aged 14 or older who consent to disclosure and international transfer to our advertising partner. On devices where the user is under 14 or did not answer the age question, or did not consent or withdrew consent, the advertising SDK is not created and no ads are shown. If you withdraw consent, new ad requests and preloading stop immediately, and any ad already loaded or being loaded is discarded. An ad that is already playing plays to the end, and its reward is given. An advertising SDK already initialized during that session is not released until you close the app, but it makes no new ad requests, and from the next launch the advertising SDK is not initialized. Information already sent to Google is processed by Google and can be managed as described below.

The Google advertising SDK automatically collects the items in Section 3, that is, behavioral information such as app and ad interactions and device identifiers. Personalized ads are handled differently by age.

  • Ages 14–15: every ad request and Google privacy choice request is tagged "under the age of consent" (TagForUnderAgeOfConsent). For requests with this tag, Google does not use personalized advertising including remarketing, or requests to third-party ad vendors such as ad measurement pixels and third-party ad servers (Google documentation).

In regions where Google requires a privacy choice screen, such as the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and some U.S. states, your choice there is followed.
  • A maximum ad content rating of "PG" is applied to every ad request, so ads rated higher than that are not requested.
  • The iOS version does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, so the advertising SDK does not access the IDFA. Ad performance is measured with Apple's SKAdNetwork, in which Apple aggregates ad conversions and reports them to ad networks without identifying individuals or devices.

Ways to refuse and manage:

12. In-app purchases

We sell 13 non-consumable products: "Skip ads", "Supporter" (a supporter label), the "Welcome bundle" combining both, 5 outfits and 5 faces. Payments are processed through Google Play billing on Android and through Apple's App Store in-app purchase on iOS. In Korea, Google Play payments by domestic card, mobile phone billing and similar are processed by Google Payment Korea Ltd., and other payment methods such as foreign cards by the Google entity under Google's payments terms (Google Payments buyer terms). App Store payments are processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. Payment method information is not passed to the Game or the Company.

The Game processes purchases with Unity IAP 5.4.4. On Android, it receives the product ID, purchase token and purchase receipt from Google Play and verifies the signature on the device with the Google Play public key. On iOS, it checks on the device the Apple-signed transaction information (transaction ID and product ID) confirmed by StoreKit. Receipts are not sent to the Company's game server for verification, and no purchase records are kept on the Company's server. Only the purchase entitlement records in Section 1 are stored on the device. Information Unity IAP sends to Unity during purchases is described in Section 3.

Restoring purchases re-queries and verifies the Google Play purchase history of the same Google account or the App Store purchase history of the same Apple account. On a device that has completed policy consent and the age check (14 or older), this happens when the Game starts, when you enter the shop, when you return to the app for a reason other than an ad, and when you tap "Restore purchases" in the shop. On iOS, "Restore purchases" first syncs transactions with the App Store and then queries them. When a refund or cancellation is confirmed, the entitlement is no longer applied. Withdrawals, refunds and cancellations of minors' purchases are governed by Article 5-3 and Article 5-4 of the Terms of Service.

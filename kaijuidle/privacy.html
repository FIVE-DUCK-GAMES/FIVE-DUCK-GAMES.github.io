Chonky Kaiju Privacy Policy

FIVE DUCK GAMES provides Chonky Kaiju (와장창 괴수, com.fiveduckgames.kaijuidle). It is available on Google Play (Android) and the App Store (iOS). Contact: fiveduckgames@gmail.com. Last updated: October 27, 2026.

You can play Chonky Kaiju without creating an account. Your progress is saved on your device, an in-game ranking shows your best stage under a generated nickname, and on Android you can optionally sign in with Google Play Games to join its leaderboards and keep a cloud save. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional in-app purchases through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iOS. It uses Google Firebase to measure how the game is played and to collect crash reports. We do not run servers of our own (the ranking is hosted by Unity Gaming Services) and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Game data on your device

Gold, evolution stones, the stages you have reached, upgrade levels, the kaiju forms you have unlocked and their mastery, mission and weekly progress, boost time left, the time you last played (for offline rewards), the list of products you own and the music, sound effect, vibration and auto-upgrade settings are stored in the app’s storage on your device, together with a checksum that detects damaged or edited saves. They are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up app data to your Google account under Google’s settings and policies; we cannot access these backups. On iOS, app data may be included in your iCloud or device backups under Apple’s settings and policies; we cannot access these backups either. If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders (for example when offline rewards are full) as local notifications on your device. Notification contents are not sent anywhere; only whether you allowed notifications and whether you opened one are recorded as analytics events (section 5). You can turn them off in your device settings. The vibration permission is used only for short in-game haptic feedback, which you can turn off in settings.

2. In-game ranking (Unity Gaming Services)

After you beat your first boss, the game signs you in anonymously to Unity Gaming Services (Authentication and Leaderboards), operated by Unity Technologies. No account, name or email is asked for. Unity creates a random player ID and a sign-in token stored on your device, and the game gives you a randomly generated nickname (for example “Sleepy Octopus#1234”), which you can re-roll once a day. Your best stage is submitted to the ranking, and other players see your nickname, best stage and rank. Unity may also process your IP address and device and app information to provide the service. Clearing app data or reinstalling starts a new anonymous player; the old entry stays on the board. To have your ranking entry deleted, contact us with your nickname. This data is kept until the ranking is reset or you ask for deletion, and Unity handles it under the Unity Privacy Policy.

2b. Google Play Games (Android only, optional sign-in)

When the game starts, Google Play Games may sign you in automatically in the background, and you can also sign in from settings; you can keep playing without signing in. When you are signed in, Google shares your Play Games player ID, gamer name and avatar with the game. Your highest stage and the number of buildings you have smashed are submitted to Google Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see them together with your gamer name according to your Play Games profile settings. A copy of your game data (section 1) is also kept as a Play Games saved game in a hidden app folder of your Google account, so you can continue after reinstalling or on another device. You can manage your profile visibility or delete your Play Games data for this game in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings. Google Play Games is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (Starter Pack, Remove Ads, Convenience Pack and Rage Pass, which are one-time products, and evolution stone packs, which are delivered at once) are made through Google Play billing; the game talks to Google Play through the Unity In-App Purchasing library. Google collects your payment details directly, and we never receive them. The purchase information Google Play delivers to your device (product ID, order ID, purchase token, purchase time and state) is checked on your device to grant and restore the products you bought, and the products you own are read again from Google Play when the game starts, so a refunded product stops working. Order IDs of evolution stone packs are kept with your game data so a purchase is never granted twice. We do not send this information to servers of our own. Payments, refunds and transaction records follow Google Play’s policies and are not deleted when you clear app data or uninstall. The Unity In-App Purchasing library may send technical diagnostics about the purchase flow to Unity under Unity’s privacy policy.

On iOS, the same products are sold through Apple’s App Store In-App Purchase, using the same library. Apple collects your payment details directly, and we never receive them. The transaction information the App Store provides to your device (product ID, transaction ID, purchase date and state) is checked on your device to grant your products, and you can use Restore in the shop to restore one-time products bought with the same Apple Account. Transaction IDs of evolution stone packs are kept with your game data so a purchase is never granted twice. Payments, refunds and transaction records follow Apple’s policies.

4. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Rewarded ads play only when you tap an ad button, for example to add boost time, triple an offline reward or a supply crate, or double evolution stones and mission rewards. Full-screen ads may appear after an evolution or after conquering a city, only after your second evolution and 30 minutes into play, and at most once every 4 minutes; Remove Ads and Rage Pass turn them off. Where consent is required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), your advertising choices are collected with Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and you can change them later with the ad privacy settings button in the game’s settings. To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect your advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, device and operating system information, app information, ad interactions such as views and clicks, crash and performance data of the SDK, and (where permitted) information used for personalized ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. On iOS, the game does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or its SDKs and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites; the SDK may use Apple’s identifier for vendors and device information for ad delivery, frequency capping and fraud prevention.

5. Analytics and crash reports (Google Firebase)

  • Google Analytics for Firebase collects an app instance ID, your advertising ID where available and permitted, device model, operating system, language, approximate country and region derived from your IP address, app version, and session and engagement information. We also record game events such as ad views and their placement, purchase results, evolution stones received and similar progress events. We use this to balance the game and the ads and to fix problems. It does not include your name, email address or gamer name. In the regions listed in section 4, analytics and ad measurement follow the choices you made in the consent message.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports, including stack traces, device model, operating system, app version, device state at the time of the crash and a Crashlytics installation ID, so we can fix bugs.

6. Websites and support

GitHub Pages hosts these policy pages; requests may expose your IP address and request metadata to the host. Support emails contain your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send; we keep them as long as needed to resolve your request and delete them on request. Do not send passwords or full payment details.

7. Legal bases, retention and transfers

Processing relies, as applicable, on providing the game you asked for, our legitimate interests in operating and funding a free game (including advertising), legal obligations, and your consent where required. Game data on your device stays until you clear app data; in-game ranking entries stay until the ranking is reset or you ask us to delete yours; Play Games leaderboard entries and saved games (Android) stay until you delete your Play Games data; data held by Google, Apple and Unity is kept under their policies. Processing may take place outside your country (for example in the United States), with transfer safeguards described by each provider, including EU standard contractual clauses where applicable.

8. Your rights and children

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before. You can reset your advertising ID, change ad choices in settings, delete your Play Games data as described in section 2, clear the app’s data, or contact us. We do not hold data that identifies you by name, so we may ask for information such as your Play Games gamer name to find records we can act on.

Chonky Kaiju is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the store’s data safety information before we change what the game collects. Material changes will be announced on this page with a new date.

Cube Digger Privacy Policy

FIVE DUCK GAMES provides Cube Digger (com.fiveduckgames.cubedigger). Contact: fiveduckgames@gmail.com. Last updated: October 2, 2026.

You can play Cube Digger without creating an account. Your progress is saved on your device, and you can optionally sign in with Google Play Games to join leaderboards. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, sells optional in-app purchases through Google Play, and uses Google Firebase to measure how the game is played, collect crash reports and adjust game settings. We do not run our own servers and do not sell your personal information.

1. Game data on your device

Coins, boosters, the levels you have cleared and their stars, the treasures in your Treasure Book, the list of products you own, daily free-booster counts and the sound, music, vibration and language settings are stored in the app’s storage on your device. They are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up app data to your Google account under Google’s settings and policies; we cannot access these backups. The vibration permission is used only for short in-game haptic feedback, which you can turn off in settings.

2. Google Play Games (optional sign-in)

When the game starts it may try to sign you in to Google Play Games in the background; you can keep playing without signing in. When you are signed in, Google shares your Play Games player ID, gamer name and avatar with the game, and the number of levels you have cleared and your total stars are submitted to Google Play Games leaderboards, where other players can see them together with your gamer name according to your Play Games profile settings. There is no cloud save. You can manage your profile visibility or delete your Play Games data for this game in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings. Google Play Games is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (coin packs, a booster bundle, a starter pack and Remove Ads) are made through Google Play billing; the game talks to Google Play through the Unity In-App Purchasing library. Google collects your payment details directly, and we never receive them. The purchase information Google Play delivers to your device (product ID, order ID, purchase token, purchase time and state) is verified on your device to grant and restore the items you bought, and transaction IDs are kept with your game data to prevent duplicate grants. We do not send it to servers of our own. Payments, refunds and transaction records follow Google Play’s policies and are not deleted when you clear app data or uninstall. Purchase events (the product and the result) are also recorded in Firebase Analytics (section 5). The Unity In-App Purchasing library may send technical diagnostics about the purchase flow to Unity under Unity’s privacy policy.

4. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Rewarded ads play only when you tap an ad button, for example to get five more shovels, double the coins from a cleared level or get a free booster. Full-screen ads may appear between levels; buying Remove Ads turns them off. Where consent is required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), your advertising choices are collected with Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and you can change them later with the Privacy Options button in settings.

The ads SDK may process your device’s advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device, operating system and app information, ad impressions, clicks and completions, and error and performance diagnostics, for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalized ads. See Google’s policies for details, retention and processing locations. To limit personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

5. Analytics, crash reports and remote settings (Google Firebase)

  • Google Analytics for Firebase collects an app instance ID, your advertising ID where available and permitted, device model, operating system, language, approximate country and region derived from your IP address, app version, and session and engagement information. We also record game events such as levels started and finished, stars, shovels used, continues, boosters used, ad views, shop visits and purchase results, Treasure Book rewards, settings changes and sign-in results. We use this to find where players get stuck, balance levels and ads, and fix problems. It does not include your name, email address or gamer name. In the regions listed in section 4, analytics and ad measurement follow the choices you made in the consent message.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports, including stack traces, device model, operating system, app version, device state at the time of the crash and a Crashlytics installation ID, so we can fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config uses a Firebase installation ID to download game settings such as ad frequency and daily free boosters.

Firebase processes this data on Google’s servers, which may be outside your country. Analytics data is kept according to the data retention setting of our Google Analytics property and then deleted; crash reports are kept for 90 days. Diagnostics and analytics in the Unity game engine are turned off.

6. Websites and support

GitHub Pages hosts these policy pages; requests may expose your IP address and request metadata to the host. Support emails contain your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send; we keep them as long as needed to resolve your request and delete them on request. Do not send passwords or full payment details.

7. Legal bases, retention and transfers

Processing relies, as applicable, on providing the game you asked for, our legitimate interests in operating, improving and funding a free game (including analytics and advertising), legal obligations, and your consent where required. Game data on your device stays until you clear app data; leaderboard entries stay until you delete your Play Games data; data held by Google and Unity is kept under their policies. Processing may take place outside your country (for example in the United States), with transfer safeguards described by each provider, including EU standard contractual clauses where applicable.

8. Your rights and children

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before. You can reset your advertising ID, change ad choices in settings, delete your Play Games data as described in section 2, clear the app’s data, or contact us. We do not hold data that identifies you by name, so we may ask for information such as your Play Games gamer name to find records we can act on.

Cube Digger is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the store’s data safety information before we change what the game collects. Material changes will be announced on this page with a new date.

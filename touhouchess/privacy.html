TOUHOU CHESS Privacy Policy

FIVE DUCK GAMES provides TOUHOU CHESS on Steam (PC) and on Google Play and the App Store (Android package and iOS bundle ID com.fiveduckgames.touhouchess). Contact: fiveduckgames@gmail.com. Effective and last updated: November 21, 2026.

TOUHOU CHESS is an unofficial fan game of Touhou Project. You can play it without an account, and your progress is saved on your device. Online play is optional and uses Epic Online Services (EOS). We do not run a game server. In online play your name comes from Steam, Google Play Games or Game Center, and your Quick match ratings are kept only on your device. On phones, the game shows ads from Google AdMob, sells one optional in-app purchase (Remove Ads) through Google Play or the App Store, and uses Google Firebase for analytics, crash reports and remote settings. We never ask for your real name, email address or phone number, and we do not sell your personal information.

1. Game data on your device

Your story and puzzle progress, puzzle rating, records against each guest, spell cards and decks, recent games kept for review, online settings and records (whether your flag is shown, the name the game made up for you if one is used, your Quick match ratings, and your online wins, losses and draws), other settings (language, sound, display), the number of full-screen ads you have seen and when the last one appeared, and whether you own Remove Ads are stored in the app’s storage on your device. They are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall the game. If device backup is enabled, the operating system may back up app data to your Google or iCloud account under the platform’s settings and policies; we cannot access these backups. On PC, the save file may also be backed up to Steam Cloud (section 6).

2. Online play (Epic Online Services)

  • Device sign-in: when you first open the online screen, the game signs in to EOS, provided by Epic Games, with an anonymous login (Device ID) created for each device. When the login is created, the game sends your device’s model name to Epic. You do not need an Epic account. EOS gives the game an account ID (product user ID) for this device, which the game uses to connect you with your opponent.
  • Lobbies: to find an opponent or open a friend room, the game creates or searches EOS lobbies. A lobby holds the game, protocol and app versions, the rules, time control and handicap, the room code of a friend match, and for Quick match the queue and your Quick match rating. Other players’ games can read it while searching for opponents.
  • Match connections: games are played between the two devices over an EOS peer-to-peer connection, which EOS relays through its own servers when needed. The two devices exchange your platform name, the platform you play on, your country (only if your flag is shown), your character and spell card deck, your Quick match rating, your app version, the moves of the game, requests such as draw offers and rematches, and quick-chat messages (fixed phrases). A direct connection lets your opponent’s device see your IP address. EOS sees your IP address in any case.
  • No game server: we do not run a game server, and we do not receive or store information from online play, such as names, ratings or games. Epic Games processes EOS data under the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

3. Your online name (Steam, Google Play Games and Game Center)

Online play uses the name from the platform you play on: your Steam name on PC, your Google Play Games name on Android and your Game Center name on iOS. On phones, the game signs in to Google Play Games or Game Center when it starts, if the platform allows it, and the platform shares your player ID and name with the game; the game uses only the name, as your name in online play. If the platform gives no name (for example, because you are not signed in) or the name contains a banned word, the game uses a name it makes up instead and keeps that name on your device. Names are checked for banned words only on your device and are not sent to us. To change your name or sign out, use the settings of Steam, Google Play Games or Game Center. Data held by these services is processed by Valve, Google and Apple under their own privacy policies.

4. Quick match ratings and online record

Quick match has a rating for each queue (spell cards and normal chess). Ratings are calculated on your device from the results of your Quick match games and are stored only on your device, together with your online record (wins, losses and draws). Your starting rating depends on your answer to the question asked at first launch. Friend matches are not rated. We do not receive your ratings or records. Your rating is sent to other players’ games through lobbies and match connections (section 2) so that players of similar strength can be matched and the rating can be shown. On PC, the save file that contains your ratings may also be backed up to Steam Cloud (section 6). You can reset them with Settings > Online > Delete online data.

5. What other players can see

  • Opponents see your platform name, the platform you play on (Steam, Android or iOS, shown as an icon), your country flag, your character and spell card deck, your Quick match rating, your moves and quick-chat messages. Their game also receives your app version and your EOS account ID, which is not displayed.
  • Country flag: your country is determined on your device: on PC from the country Steam determines from your IP address, on iOS from the App Store country, and on Android from the SIM or mobile network country; never from your device language or GPS. If it cannot be determined, no flag is shown. If you turn off “Show my flag to opponents” in Settings > Online, your country is not sent to opponents.
  • Lobbies: the games of players who are searching for an opponent can read the lobby information in section 2, including your Quick match rating.
  • Practice bot: if no opponent is found in time in Quick match, you may be matched with a practice bot (AI). The bot plays on your device, uses a made-up name, platform and flag, and is not a real player.
  • Steam friends can see your Steam rich presence, for example which guest you are playing or that you are in an online match, and while you wait in a friend room they can join it from their friends list or from an invitation you send.

6. Steam (PC)

The Steam version uses Valve’s Steamworks: your Steam ID and Steam name (used as your name in online play), the country Steam determines from your IP address (used for your flag), achievements, rich presence for your friends list, invitations to friend rooms that you send through the Steam overlay (they carry the room code), and Steam Cloud copies of your save file (including your online record and ratings). Purchases of the game are handled by Steam. Valve processes this data under the Steam Privacy Policy. The Steam version shows no ads and does not use Firebase.

7. In-app purchase (phones)

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play billing or the Apple App Store; the game talks to the store through the Unity In-App Purchasing library. The store collects your payment details directly, and we never receive them. The purchase information the store delivers to your device (product ID, order or transaction ID, purchase token or receipt, purchase time and state) is checked on your device to turn ads off and to restore the purchase, and the fact that you own Remove Ads is kept with your game data. We do not receive it, and it is not synced between devices. Payments, refunds and transaction records follow the store’s policies. The Unity In-App Purchasing library may send technical diagnostics about the purchase flow to Unity under Unity’s privacy policy.

8. Advertising (phones)

The mobile version uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show full-screen ads between games, for example after you leave a finished match or finish a story chapter. Ads never appear during a game. Buying Remove Ads turns them off. Where consent is required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), your advertising choices are collected with Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and you can change them later in settings. On iOS, the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before the first ad request; if you decline, the advertising identifier is not used.

The ads SDK may process your device’s advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device, operating system and app information, ad impressions and clicks, and error and performance diagnostics, for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalized ads. See Google’s policies for details, retention and processing locations. To limit personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

9. Analytics, crash reports and remote settings (Google Firebase, phones only)

  • Google Analytics for Firebase collects an app instance ID, your advertising ID where available and permitted, device model, operating system, language, approximate country and region derived from your IP address, app version, and session and engagement information. We also record game events such as your answer to the first-launch question and tutorial progress, your answer to the iOS tracking prompt, the result of signing in to Google Play Games or Game Center, Quick match searches and practice bot matches (the queue and your Quick match rating), full-screen ads shown, Remove Ads offers shown, tapped or closed, purchase results with price and currency, the number of finished games and days since install. We use this to improve the game, set ad frequency and fix problems. It does not include your name, email address, platform name or online account ID. In the regions listed in section 8, analytics and ad measurement follow the choices you made in the consent message.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports, including stack traces, device model, operating system, app version, device state at the time of the crash and a Crashlytics installation ID, so we can fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config uses a Firebase installation ID to download game settings such as how often full-screen ads may appear.

Firebase processes this data on Google’s servers, which may be outside your country. The Steam version does not use Firebase.

7. 인앱 구매(휴대폰)

광고 제거는 Google Play 결제 또는 Apple App Store로 하는 1회 구매이며, 게임은 Unity In-App Purchasing 라이브러리로 스토어와 통신합니다. 결제 정보는 스토어가 직접 받으며 개발자는 받지 않습니다. 스토어가 기기로 보내는 구매 정보(상품 ID, 주문 또는 거래 ID, 구매 토큰 또는 영수증, 구매 시각과 상태)는 광고를 끄고 구매를 복원하기 위해 기기에서 확인하며, 광고 제거를 가졌다는 사실은 게임 데이터와 함께 저장됩니다. 개발자는 이 정보를 받지 않으며, 기기 사이에서 동기화하지도 않습니다. 결제, 환불, 거래 기록은 스토어 정책을 따릅니다. Unity In-App Purchasing 라이브러리는 구매 과정의 기술 진단 정보를 Unity 개인정보 처리방침에 따라 Unity에 보낼 수 있습니다.

8. 광고(휴대폰)

휴대폰 판은 Google AdMob(Google Mobile Ads SDK)으로 대국과 대국 사이에 전면 광고를 보여 줍니다. 예를 들어 끝난 대국을 나갈 때나 이야기 한 화를 마쳤을 때입니다. 대국 중에는 광고가 나오지 않으며, 광고 제거를 사면 광고가 꺼집니다. 법이 동의를 요구하는 지역(예: EEA, 영국, 스위스)에서는 광고를 요청하기 전에 Google의 사용자 메시지 플랫폼(UMP)으로 광고 선택을 받으며, 나중에 설정에서 바꿀 수 있습니다. iOS에서는 첫 광고 요청 전에 추적 허용(App Tracking Transparency)을 묻고, 거부하면 광고 식별자를 쓰지 않습니다.

Kjøpsopplysningene butikken sender til enheten (produkt-ID, ordre- eller transaksjons-ID, kjøpstoken eller kvittering, kjøpstidspunkt og status), kontrolleres på enheten for å slå av reklame og gjenopprette kjøpet, og at du eier Fjern reklame, lagres sammen med spilldataene. Vi mottar ikke dette, og det synkroniseres ikke mellom enheter. Betaling, refusjon og transaksjonsregistreringer følger butikkens retningslinjer. Biblioteket Unity In-App Purchasing kan sende teknisk diagnostikk om kjøpsflyten til Unity etter Unitys personvernerklæring.

8. Reklame (telefon)

Mobilversjonen bruker Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) til å vise fullskjermannonser mellom partier, for eksempel når du forlater et ferdig parti eller fullfører et kapittel i historien. Det vises aldri reklame under et parti. Kjøper du Fjern reklame, slås reklamen av. Der loven krever samtykke (for eksempel i EØS, Storbritannia og Sveits), innhentes reklamevalgene dine med Googles brukermeldingsplattform (UMP) før det bes om annonser, og du kan endre dem senere i innstillingene. På iOS ber spillet om tillatelse til sporing (App Tracking Transparency) før første annonseforespørsel; avslår du, brukes ikke reklameidentifikatoren.

Annonse-SDK-en kan behandle enhetens reklame-ID, IP-adresse og omtrentlig posisjon utledet av den, opplysninger om enhet, operativsystem og app, annonsevisninger og -klikk, og diagnostikk om feil og ytelse, til levering av annonser, frekvensbegrensning, måling, svindelforebygging og, der det er tillatt, tilpasset reklame. Se Googles retningslinjer for detaljer, lagringstid og behandlingssteder. For å begrense tilpasset reklame kan du tilbakestille eller slette reklame-ID-en i enhetens innstillinger.

9. Analyse, krasjrapporter og fjerninnstillinger (Google Firebase, bare telefon)

  • Google Analytics for Firebase samler inn en appforekomst-ID, reklame-ID-en din der den er tilgjengelig og tillatt, enhetsmodell, operativsystem, språk, omtrentlig land og region utledet av IP-adressen, appversjon og opplysninger om økter og engasjement. Vi registrerer også spillhendelser, som svaret ditt på spørsmålet ved første oppstart og fremgang i opplæringen, svaret ditt på sporingsforespørselen i iOS, resultatet av påloggingen til Google Play Spill eller Game Center, søk etter rask kamp og partier mot treningsboten (køen og ratingen din i rask kamp), viste fullskjermannonser, når tilbud om Fjern reklame vises, trykkes på eller lukkes, kjøpsresultater med pris og valuta, antall fullførte partier og dager siden installasjon. Vi bruker dette til å forbedre spillet, justere reklamefrekvensen og rette feil. Det inkluderer ikke navnet, e-postadressen, plattformnavnet eller nettkonto-ID-en din. I områdene nevnt i punkt 8 følger analyse og annonsemåling valgene du tok i samtykkemeldingen.
  • Firebase Crashlytics samler inn krasj- og feilrapporter, blant annet stakksporing, enhetsmodell, operativsystem, appversjon, enhetens tilstand ved krasjet og en Crashlytics-installasjons-ID, slik at vi kan rette feil.
  • Firebase Remote Config bruker en Firebase-installasjons-ID til å laste ned spillinnstillinger, for eksempel hvor ofte fullskjermannonser kan vises.

Firebase behandler disse dataene på Googles servere, som kan ligge utenfor landet ditt. Steam-versjonen bruker ikke Firebase.

TOUHOU CHESS Privacy Policy

FIVE DUCK GAMES provides TOUHOU CHESS (Android package and iOS bundle ID com.fiveduckgames.touhouchess). Contact: fiveduckgames@gmail.com. Last updated: November 19, 2026.

TOUHOU CHESS is an unofficial fan game of Touhou Project. You can play it without creating an account, and your progress is saved on your device. Online matches use Epic Online Services. The mobile version shows ads from Google AdMob and sells one optional in-app purchase (Remove Ads) through Google Play or the App Store. We do not run our own game servers and do not sell your personal information.

1. Game data on your device

Your story and puzzle progress, puzzle rating, records against each guest, spell cards and decks, online match record, settings (language, sound, display), the number of full-screen ads you have seen and when the last one appeared, and whether you own Remove Ads are stored in the app’s storage on your device. They are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. If device backup is enabled, the operating system may back up app data to your Google or iCloud account under the platform’s settings and policies; we cannot access these backups.

2. Online matches (Epic Online Services)

Online matches are optional. When you open the online screen, the game signs in to Epic Online Services (EOS), provided by Epic Games, with an anonymous device credential that EOS creates on your device. You do not need an Epic account. EOS gives the game a product user ID for this device.

  • Lobbies: to find an opponent or open a friend room, the game creates or searches EOS lobbies that hold the game version, the rules and time control you chose, and the room code.
  • During a match: the two players exchange their display name (section 3), the character they play as, their app version and the moves of the game over an EOS peer-to-peer connection. EOS may relay this connection through its servers. A direct connection lets your opponent’s device see your IP address, and EOS sees your IP address in either case.
  • We do not receive or store match data on servers of our own. Your online record is kept only on your device (section 1). EOS processes data under the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

3. Display name: Google Play Games and Game Center

On Android the game signs in to Google Play Games, and on iOS to Apple Game Center, when the platform allows it. The platform shares your player ID and your gamer name (Play Games) or nickname (Game Center) with the game. The game uses that name only as your display name in online matches, where your opponent sees it. If you are not signed in, a default name is used. You can change your gamer name or nickname, or sign out, in Google Play Games or Game Center settings. These services are operated by Google and Apple under their own privacy policies.

4. In-app purchase

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play billing or the Apple App Store; the game talks to the store through the Unity In-App Purchasing library. The store collects your payment details directly, and we never receive them. The purchase information the store delivers to your device (product ID, order or transaction ID, purchase token or receipt, purchase time and state) is checked on your device to turn ads off and to restore the purchase, and the fact that you own Remove Ads is kept with your game data. We do not send it to servers of our own. Payments, refunds and transaction records follow the store’s policies. The Unity In-App Purchasing library may send technical diagnostics about the purchase flow to Unity under Unity’s privacy policy.

5. Advertising

The mobile version uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show full-screen ads between games, for example after you leave a finished match or finish a story chapter. Ads never appear during a game. Buying Remove Ads turns them off. Where consent is required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), your advertising choices are collected with Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and you can change them later in settings. On iOS, the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before the first ad request; if you decline, the advertising identifier is not used.

The ads SDK may process your device’s advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device, operating system and app information, ad impressions and clicks, and error and performance diagnostics, for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalized ads. See Google’s policies for details, retention and processing locations. To limit personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

6. Game settings file, websites and support

The game downloads a small settings file (for example how often full-screen ads may appear) from this website, which is hosted on GitHub Pages. Requests to GitHub Pages, including visits to these policy pages, may expose your IP address and request metadata to the host. Support emails contain your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send; we keep them as long as needed to resolve your request and delete them on request. Do not send passwords or full payment details. Diagnostics and analytics in the Unity game engine are turned off.

7. Legal bases, retention and transfers

Processing relies, as applicable, on providing the game and the online matches you asked for, our legitimate interests in operating, improving and funding a free game (including advertising), legal obligations, and your consent where required. Game data on your device stays until you clear app data; data held by Epic Games, Google, Apple and Unity is kept under their policies. Processing may take place outside your country (for example in the United States), with transfer safeguards described by each provider, including EU standard contractual clauses where applicable.

8. Your rights and children

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before. You can reset your advertising ID, change ad choices in settings, sign out of Google Play Games or Game Center, clear the app’s data, or contact us. We do not hold data that identifies you by name, so we may ask for information such as your display name to find records we can act on. See also how to delete your data.

TOUHOU CHESS is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us.

9. Changes

We will update this page and the stores’ privacy information before we change what the game collects. Material changes will be announced on this page with a new date.

Delete your TOUHOU CHESS data

TOUHOU CHESS by FIVE DUCK GAMES (Steam; com.fiveduckgames.touhouchess on Google Play and the App Store). You can delete your online account in the game at any time, or ask us by email if you can no longer open the game. We handle requests within 10 days.

Delete your online account in the game

  1. Open the game, make sure you are connected to the internet, and go to Online.
  2. Open Account and choose Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted and confirm.
  • Android: follow the steps above. If you linked Google, you can also remove TOUHOU CHESS from your Google Account under Third-party apps and services.
  • iOS: follow the steps above. If you used Sign in with Apple, you can also stop using it for TOUHOU CHESS in Settings > [your name] > Sign-In & Security > Sign in with Apple. If you linked Google, see the Android note.
  • Steam (PC): follow the steps above. The link to your Steam account is removed; your Steam account, your copy of the game and your achievements are not affected. If you linked Google, see the Android note.

The account is deleted for every device that uses it.

What is deleted

  • On our game server: your profile (nickname, #tag, country, character), the type of linked account, your Ranked rating and hidden matchmaking ratings, fair-play and sanction records, the reports you filed and your own result reports. You disappear from leaderboards at once.
  • At Epic Online Services: your cloud save in Player Data Storage, the links to your Google, Apple and Steam accounts, and this device’s anonymous device credential.

What is kept

  • Match records stay so that other players’ ratings remain correct, but your account ID in them is replaced with a random anonymous ID. Reports other players filed about you are kept in the same way.
  • Request logs, which contain your account ID, are deleted after 14 days, and backups no longer contain your data after about 8 days.
  • Your progress on this device is not deleted. To delete it, use Settings > Reset progress, clear the app’s data or uninstall the game. On Steam, Steam Cloud may keep a copy of the save file.
  • Epic Games, Google, Apple and Valve may keep some records under their own policies.

Deleted ratings cannot be restored. If you play online again later, a new account with a new #tag is created.

Ask us to delete your data

If you cannot open the game any more (for example, you lost your device), email fiveduckgames@gmail.com with the subject “TOUHOU CHESS data deletion” and include:

  • your nickname and #tag (for example Name#1234);
  • your platform (Android, iOS or Steam);
  • roughly when you last played online.

To protect players from requests made by someone else, we may ask you to confirm that the account is yours, for example by temporarily changing your nickname in the game to a code we send you. After checking, we delete the data on our game server listed above and confirm by email within 10 days.

We cannot remove data that only Epic Games holds (the account links and the cloud save) from our side. If you can still sign in with your linked Google, Apple or Steam account on any device, use the in-game deletion, which removes them as well. Otherwise you can contact Epic Games under the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

Other data

  • Game progress and settings: stored on your device. Clear the app’s data in your device settings, uninstall the game, or use Settings > Reset progress.
  • Advertising: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Analytics and crash reports (phones): not linked to your nickname or online account, and kept for the periods in the Privacy Policy. Clearing the app’s data gives the game a new app instance ID.
  • Purchases: transaction records are kept by Google Play, the App Store or Steam under their policies.

For any other request, email fiveduckgames@gmail.com with the subject “TOUHOU CHESS data deletion”. We reply within 10 days.

Удалённые рейтинги восстановить нельзя. Если позже вы снова будете играть онлайн, будет создана новая учётная запись с новым #тегом.

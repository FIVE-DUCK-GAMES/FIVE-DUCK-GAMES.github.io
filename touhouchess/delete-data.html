Delete your TOUHOU CHESS data

TOUHOU CHESS by FIVE DUCK GAMES (Steam; com.fiveduckgames.touhouchess on Google Play and the App Store). We do not run a game server. The information online play uses is kept only on your device (on PC, also in its Steam Cloud backup) and at Epic Online Services (this device’s anonymous login), and you can delete it in the game at any time. We take other requests by email and handle them within 10 days. Effective and last updated: November 21, 2026.

Delete your online data in the game

  1. Open the game while connected to the internet, and open Settings.
  2. On the Online tab, choose Delete… next to Delete online data. This row appears once the online screen has been opened on this device, and it cannot be used during a game.
  3. Read the message and choose Delete.

The steps are the same on Android, iOS and Steam (PC). The deletion applies only to this device.

What is deleted

  • At Epic Online Services: this device’s anonymous login (Device ID).
  • On this device: your online record (wins, losses and draws), Quick match ratings and the name the game made up for you. The flag setting returns to its default (shown).

Deleted ratings and records cannot be restored. If you play online again, a new anonymous login is created and your ratings start over.

What is kept

  • Your progress and settings on this device (story, puzzles, records against guests, decks and so on). To delete them, use Settings > General > Reset progress, clear the app’s data or uninstall the game.
  • Your Steam, Google Play Games or Game Center account and name: these accounts are separate from the game and are not affected. To change or delete them, use the settings of each service.
  • Your Remove Ads purchase stays.
  • Steam Cloud: copies of the PC save file are kept under Steam’s settings and policies.
  • Epic Games, Google, Apple and Valve may keep some records under their own policies.

Uninstalling the game

Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes the game data stored on your device, including your progress, online record and ratings. To delete the anonymous login at Epic Online Services as well, use Delete online data before you uninstall. Device backups (Google account, iCloud) and Steam Cloud may keep copies under the settings of each service.

Other data

  • Advertising: reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Analytics and crash reports (phones): not linked to your platform name or online account, and kept for the periods in the Privacy Policy. Clearing the app’s data gives the game a new app instance ID.
  • Purchases: transaction records are kept by Google Play, the App Store or Steam under their policies.

Ask us by email

We do not run a game server and hold no information from your online play, so there is no online data for us to look up or delete on our side. If you can no longer open the game (for example, you lost your device) and want the anonymous login at Epic Games deleted, you can ask Epic Games under the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

For any other request, email fiveduckgames@gmail.com with the subject “TOUHOU CHESS data deletion” and your platform (Android, iOS or Steam). We reply within 10 days.

Dette beholdes

  • Fremgangen og innstillingene på denne enheten (historie, oppgaver, resultater mot gjester, kortstokker og så videre). For å slette dem kan du bruke Innstillinger > Generelt > Nullstill fremgangen, tømme appdataene eller avinstallere spillet.
  • Kontoen og navnet ditt i Steam, Google Play Spill eller Game Center: disse kontoene er atskilt fra spillet og påvirkes ikke. For å endre eller slette dem kan du bruke innstillingene i hver tjeneste.
  • Kjøpet av Fjern reklame beholdes.
  • Steam Cloud: kopier av lagringsfilen fra PC oppbevares etter Steams innstillinger og retningslinjer.
  • Epic Games, Google, Apple og Valve kan beholde enkelte registreringer etter egne retningslinjer.

Når du avinstallerer spillet

Avinstallerer du spillet eller tømmer appdataene, slettes spilldataene som er lagret på enheten, også fremgangen, resultatene og ratingene på nett. For også å slette den anonyme påloggingen hos Epic Online Services bruker du Slett nettdata før du avinstallerer. Sikkerhetskopier av enheten (Google-konto, iCloud) og Steam Cloud kan beholde kopier etter innstillingene i hver tjeneste.

Andre data

  • Reklame: tilbakestill eller slett reklame-ID-en i enhetens innstillinger.
  • Analyse og krasjrapporter (telefon): er ikke knyttet til plattformnavnet eller nettkontoen din, og lagres i periodene som står i personvernerklæringen. Tømmer du appdataene, får spillet en ny appforekomst-ID.
  • Kjøp: transaksjonsregistreringer oppbevares av Google Play, App Store eller Steam etter deres retningslinjer.

Be oss om noe på e-post

Vi drifter ingen spillserver og har ingen opplysninger fra nettspillet ditt, så det finnes ingen nettdata vi kan finne eller slette fra vår side. Kan du ikke lenger åpne spillet (for eksempel fordi du har mistet enheten) og vil ha slettet den anonyme påloggingen hos Epic Games, kan du kontakte Epic Games etter Epic Games’ personvernerklæring.

For andre forespørsler kan du sende en e-post til fiveduckgames@gmail.com med emnet «TOUHOU CHESS data deletion» og oppgi plattformen din (Android, iOS eller Steam). Vi svarer innen 10 dager.

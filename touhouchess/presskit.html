About

Become Rinnosuke, owner of the Kourindou curio shop, and play chess with the Gensokyo regulars who drop by. Your guests laugh, panic and grumble as the game turns. Learn in a 9-chapter story, sharpen your play with puzzles, and battle with spell cards or play friends online.

An unofficial Touhou Project fan game (original work: Team Shanghai Alice / ZUN).

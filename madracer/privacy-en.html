Mad Racer Privacy Policy

FIVE DUCK GAMES (Republic of Korea) provides Mad Racer (com.fiveduckgames.madracer) and is responsible for the personal information described here. Contact: fiveduckgames@gmail.com. Effective date: November 2, 2026.

You can play Mad Racer without creating an account. Your progress is saved on your device, and you can optionally sign in with Google Play Games to keep it in the cloud and join leaderboards. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, sells optional in-app purchases through Google Play, and uses Google Firebase to measure how the game is played, collect crash reports and adjust game settings. We do not run our own servers and do not sell your personal information.

1. Game data on your device

Coins, gems (paid and free gems are recorded separately), parts, the cars you own and their stars, paint jobs, Workshop upgrade levels, stars and records for each district, your Bounty Rank, progress on missions, the login calendar, free gifts and daily deals, weekly league results, your premium prize machine pity count and recent draws, best score and total raiders wrecked, play counts and play time, tutorial progress, the list of products you own and your purchase history (order ID, product, purchase time, unspent paid gems, refund status), when offers were last shown, your sound, music, vibration and language settings and, if you sign in, your Play Games player ID are stored in the app’s storage on your device. Scrap, levels and augments collected during a run last only for that run and are not saved. This data is removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up app data to your Google account under Google’s settings and policies; we cannot access these backups. The vibration permission is used only for short in-game haptic feedback, which you can turn off in settings.

2. Google Play Games (optional sign-in)

When the game starts it tries to sign you in to Google Play Games in the background. You can keep playing as a guest if you are not signed in. When you are signed in, Google shares your Play Games player ID, gamer name and avatar with the game, and:

  • Leaderboards: your best endless-run score is submitted to the “Best Score” leaderboard and the number of raiders you wrecked in a single run to the “Most Raiders Wrecked” leaderboard, where other players can see them together with your gamer name according to your Play Games profile settings. The weekly league reads your rank on the Best Score leaderboard for the current week to decide your weekly reward.
  • Cloud save: a copy of the game data in section 1 is stored with Google Play Games Saved Games in your Google account so you can restore it on another device. When your device and the cloud copy differ, the game merges them on your device. We do not receive it on any server of ours.

You can manage your profile visibility or delete your Play Games data for this game in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account settings. Google Play Games is operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases such as the Starter Pack, Remove Ads, gem packs and the legendary car pick are made through Google Play billing; the game talks to Google Play through the Unity In-App Purchasing library. Google collects your payment details directly, and we never receive them. The purchase information Google Play delivers to your device (product ID, order ID, purchase token, purchase time and state) is verified on your device to grant and restore the items you bought, prevent double grants and take back refunded items, and the list of products you own and your purchase history are kept with your game data (and your cloud save if you are signed in). You can see your purchase history under Settings > Purchase History. We do not send it to servers of our own. Payments, refunds and transaction records follow Google Play’s policies and are not deleted when you clear app data or uninstall. Purchase events (such as the product, whether the purchase succeeded and, for completed purchases, the price and currency) are also recorded in Firebase Analytics (section 5). The Unity In-App Purchasing library may send technical diagnostics about the purchase flow to Unity under Unity’s privacy policy.

4. Advertising

The game uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Rewarded ads play only when you tap an ad button, for example to continue a run, double your coins, start with a free augment, reroll augment cards, double a free gift or get a free prize machine draw. Full-screen ads may appear when you go from the results screen to the next run; buying Remove Ads or the Starter Pack turns them off. The game shows no banner ads. Where consent is required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), your advertising choices are collected with Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads are requested, and you can change them later with the AD CHOICES button in settings.

The ads SDK may process your device’s advertising ID, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device, operating system and app information, ad impressions, clicks and completions, and error and performance diagnostics, for ad delivery, frequency capping, measurement, fraud prevention and, where permitted, personalized ads. See Google’s policies for details, retention and processing locations. To limit personalized ads, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

5. Analytics, crash reports and remote settings (Google Firebase)

  • Google Analytics for Firebase collects an app instance ID, your advertising ID where available and permitted, device model, operating system, language, approximate country and region derived from your IP address, app version, and session and engagement information. We also record game events such as the car and district you played, score, time survived, the bounty level you reached, raiders wrecked, the augments you picked and their levels, coins earned, revives, prize machine results, Workshop upgrades, mission and login rewards, Bounty Rank and tutorial progress, ad views, shop visits, offers shown and tapped, purchase results, currency earned and spent, sign-in results, cloud save merges, your answer to the notification prompt and use of the support button. We use this to find where players get stuck, balance the game and ads, and fix problems. It does not include your name, email address or gamer name. In the regions listed in section 4, analytics and ad measurement follow the choices you made in the consent message.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports, including stack traces, device model, operating system, app version, device state at the time of the crash and a Crashlytics installation ID, so we can fix bugs.
  • Firebase Remote Config uses a Firebase installation ID to download game settings such as ad frequency, reward amounts, weekend double coins, how often offers appear and update notices.

Firebase processes this data on Google’s servers, which may be outside your country. Analytics data is kept according to the data retention setting of our Google Analytics property and then deleted; crash reports are kept for 90 days. Diagnostics and analytics in the Unity game engine are turned off.

6. Notifications, time check and support

  • Notifications (optional): the game schedules reminders on your device, for example when a free gift is ready or a login reward is waiting. On Android 13 and later the game asks in its own words first and shows the system permission prompt only if you agree. Reminders are created on the device and their content is not sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in your device settings.
  • Time check: to stop daily rewards from being collected by changing the device clock, the game sends an empty HTTPS request to a Google server (www.google.com) and reads only the current time from the response. The request carries no game data, but like any internet request it reveals your IP address and basic device information to Google.
  • Support: the CONTACT US button in settings opens your mail app with a message that already includes the app version, device model, operating system, language, your Play Games player ID (if signed in) and recent order IDs, so we can find your purchases. You can edit or remove any of it before sending. We use the emails we receive (your email address, message and any attachments) to answer your request. Do not send passwords or full payment details.
  • These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages; visiting them may expose your IP address and request metadata to GitHub.

8. Your rights and children

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and the UK, or Indonesia’s Personal Data Protection Law, Law No. 27 of 2022), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal information, to data portability, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before. You can reset your advertising ID, change ad choices in settings, turn off notifications, delete your Play Games data as described in section 2, clear the app’s data, or contact us. We do not hold data that identifies you by name, so we may ask for information such as your Play Games gamer name or an order ID to find records we can act on.

Mad Racer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us.

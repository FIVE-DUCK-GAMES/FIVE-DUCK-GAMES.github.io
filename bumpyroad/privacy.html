Bumpy Road Privacy Policy

FIVE DUCK GAMES provides Bumpy Road (com.fiveduckgames.bumpyroad). Contact: fiveduckgames@gmail.com. Last updated: September 29, 2026.

Bumpy Road is an offline game that needs no account. Your progress stays on your device, and we do not collect personal information or send it to any server. The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases and no app analytics tools.

Coins, best score, unlocked and selected cars, play counts, gift timing and sound, music and vibration settings are stored only in the app’s storage on your device. They are never sent to us and are removed when you clear the app’s data or uninstall it. If Android backup is enabled on your device, the operating system may back up app data to your Google account under Google’s settings and policies; we cannot access these backups.

The game works without an internet connection and does not send game data to any server. Diagnostics and analytics in the game engine (Unity) are turned off. The vibration permission is used only for short in-game haptic feedback, which you can turn off in settings.

Google Play handles installation, updates, test participation and install-source checks. If you allow your device to share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google may provide us with aggregated technical data such as crash reports, which do not directly identify you and are processed under Google’s privacy policy.

If we ever add ads, purchases or analytics, we will update this policy and the store’s data safety information first.

GitHub Pages hosts these policy pages; requests may expose your IP address and request metadata to the host. Support emails contain your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send; we keep them as long as needed to resolve your request and delete them on request.

Because we keep no gameplay data, clearing the app’s data removes all of it. Depending on your jurisdiction, you may request access, correction or deletion of your support correspondence by contacting us. The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect children’s personal information; guardians may contact us with any concerns. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
